This Privacy Policy explains how Speech ("we", "us", "our", or "Speech") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Speech mobile application and related services (the "Service").

Speech provides real-time speech recognition, transcription, translation, and related communication functionality.

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and operate the Service.

Depending on how you use Speech, we may collect the following categories of information.

1.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create or access an account, we may collect:

  • Apple Sign In identifier;
  • name, if provided by Apple;
  • email address, including an Apple private relay email address;
  • internal user or account identifier;
  • authentication and session information;
  • subscription-related account information.

We do not receive or store your Apple ID password.

2. Microphone and Audio Data

Speech requires access to your device microphone to provide real-time speech recognition, transcription, and translation.

When you start a translation session, the App captures audio from your device microphone and transmits the audio for real-time speech processing.

Speech does not intentionally store raw microphone audio as a permanent recording in its own database.

Audio may be processed for purposes including:

  • recognizing speech;
  • generating transcripts;
  • generating translations;
  • providing real-time AI functionality.

Audio may be transmitted to third-party technology providers, including OpenAI, when necessary to provide the requested real-time AI functionality.

You can stop microphone processing by ending a translation session or disabling microphone permission through your device settings.

3. Transcripts and Translated Text

When you use Speech, the Service may generate and store:

  • speech transcripts;
  • translated text;
  • conversation history;
  • saved translations.

These records may be associated with your account.

We use this information to provide features such as conversation history, saved translations, access to previous translations, synchronization, and other translation-related functionality.

We do not sell your transcripts or translated text.

We do not use your conversations for targeted advertising.

4. OpenAI and AI Processing

Speech uses OpenAI's API and real-time AI services to provide speech recognition, transcription, translation, and real-time AI functionality.

When you use a translation session, information necessary to provide the requested functionality may be transmitted to OpenAI.

Depending on the feature being used, this information may include:

  • audio transmitted during an active translation session;
  • speech-processing information;
  • transcript information;
  • text required to generate a translation.

OpenAI processes this information as a technology provider used by Speech to provide AI functionality.

Speech does not intentionally store raw microphone audio as a permanent recording in its own database. However, information transmitted to OpenAI may be processed and retained by OpenAI according to the applicable OpenAI terms, policies, documentation, and service configuration.

For information about how OpenAI handles API and business data, please review OpenAI's Business Data Privacy information .

15. Accuracy and Environmental Limitations

Speech is designed primarily for speech in relatively clear acoustic environments.

Speech is not intended or recommended for use in musical environments, concerts or music performances, environments with loud background music, extremely crowded environments, environments where many people are speaking simultaneously, environments with excessive background noise, or environments with significant echo or sound interference.

Background music, loud noise, overlapping speech, echoes, microphone conditions, accents, pronunciation, and network conditions may reduce speech recognition, transcription, and translation accuracy.

AI-generated transcriptions and translations may contain errors or inaccuracies. Speech should not be relied upon as the sole source of information where accuracy is critical.

For best results, use the App in a relatively quiet environment with clear speech.
